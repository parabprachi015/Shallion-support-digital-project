# Analytics Analysis and Interpretation

The available Wix Analytics data were reviewed to understand how visitors discover and use the Shallion Support website.

## Overall Traffic

During the analysed period, the website recorded 131 sessions and 95 unique visitors.

Direct traffic was the largest individual acquisition source, followed by Google organic search and Bing organic search. This indicates that both existing awareness of Shallion and search engines contribute to website discovery.

## Search Performance

The available Google search data showed strong performance for branded searches.

The query "shallion support" recorded 25 impressions and 14 clicks, with a 56% click-through rate and an average position of 1.

The query "shallion" recorded 103 impressions and 7 clicks, with a 6.8% click-through rate and an average position of 1.9.

These results indicate a strong foundation for branded search visibility. However, the available query data was mainly branded, suggesting an opportunity to improve visibility for relevant non-branded searches relating to services and support.

## Page Visibility

The homepage had the highest search visibility among the pages reviewed. Other pages, including About Us, Team, Contact Us and Volunteering, were also represented in search results.

This supports the importance of clear navigation from the homepage towards key service and information pages.

## Form Activity

The available Wix Analytics data showed 46 volunteer-form views, 7 form starts and 2 completed submissions during the analysed period.

This indicates interest in volunteering, while the lower number of completed submissions suggests that the volunteering pathway could potentially be made clearer or easier to complete.

Future evaluation should compare form views, starts and completed submissions after further website development.

## Implications for Website Development

The analytics provided a baseline that supported the wider website review and improvement process. The findings reinforced the importance of clear first-visit information, strong navigation and calls-to-action, mobile usability and future monitoring of user actions.

Future evaluation should compare the same measures after further website development to assess whether engagement and conversion pathways improve.
