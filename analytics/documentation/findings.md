# Key Findings

The website review and available analytics identified several areas relevant to future website development.

## Website Traffic

Direct traffic was the largest individual traffic source, followed by Google organic search and Bing organic search. This indicates that both existing awareness and search engines contribute to website discovery.

## Search Performance

Search performance was strongest for branded queries including "shallion support" and "shallion". This suggests that the website already has a strong foundation for users searching specifically for the organisation.

However, the available search-query data was mainly branded, indicating an opportunity to improve visibility for relevant non-branded service-related searches.

## Page Visibility

The homepage had the highest number of impressions among the pages reviewed. Other pages, including About Us, Team, Contact Us and Volunteering, also appeared in Google search results.

## User Actions

No form submissions were recorded in the period analysed. This does not demonstrate that no enquiries were made, because visitors may have used alternative contact routes such as telephone or email.

## Main Improvement Opportunity

The findings support continued work on clearer navigation, stronger calls-to-action, service information, mobile usability and measurement of user actions.
