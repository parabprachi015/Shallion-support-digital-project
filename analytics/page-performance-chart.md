# Page Performance Visualisation

The available Wix Analytics data showed that the Homepage was the most visited page during the analysed period.

| Page | Visits |
|---|---:|
| Homepage | 152 |
| About Us | 52 |
| Volunteering | 37 |
| Contact Us | 30 |

The Homepage received substantially more visits than the other pages, showing that it is an important entry point for website users.

About Us, Volunteering and Contact Us also received meaningful traffic, indicating that these pages are important parts of the user journey.

These findings supported improvements to homepage clarity, navigation, contact pathways and volunteering information. Future analytics can be used to compare page activity after the website changes.
