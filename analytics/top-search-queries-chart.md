# Top Search Queries Visualisation

The available Google search-performance data showed that the website was primarily discovered through branded searches.

| Search query | Impressions | Clicks | CTR (%) | Average position |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| shallion support | 25 | 14 | 56.0 | 1.0 |
| shallion | 103 | 7 | 6.8 | 1.9 |

The results show particularly strong performance for the branded query "shallion support", while "shallion" generated a larger number of impressions.

This suggests that Shallion has a strong foundation for branded search visibility, while future SEO work could focus on relevant non-branded searches related to chronic illness support, dementia support and volunteering.
