# Traffic Sources Visualisation

The following traffic sources were identified during the analysed period:

| Source | Sessions |
|---|---:|
| Direct | 55 |
| Google Organic Search | 48 |
| Bing Organic Search | 18 |
| Referral | 5 |
| Organic Social | 1 |
| AI Platforms | 1 |

Direct traffic was the largest individual source, while Google and Bing together represented an important proportion of website acquisition.

The data provide a baseline for evaluating how visitors discover the Shallion Support website and identifying opportunities to strengthen search, referral and social traffic.
