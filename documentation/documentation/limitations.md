# Limitations

The findings should be interpreted within the limitations of the available data and the placement timeframe.

- The analytics reviewed represent a limited period and may not reflect longer-term website behaviour.
- Wix Analytics provides platform-specific measures and definitions.
- The available search-query data was concentrated on branded searches.
- Zero recorded form submissions does not mean that no enquiries were received through other communication channels.
- Website traffic and response-status data should not be interpreted as equivalent to the number of individual users.
- The analysis was observational and did not use a controlled before-and-after study to establish causal effects of individual website changes.
- Some organisational information and materials could not be included because of confidentiality, governance or ownership considerations.
