# Evidence to Action

The website development work was informed by a combination of website review, available analytics and consideration of user pathways.

## Finding 1: Homepage visibility

The homepage was the main entry point identified in the available website analytics.

### Interpretation
Because the homepage is an important entry point, it should provide clear information about Shallion and direct visitors towards relevant services and next steps.

### Action
The homepage structure and content were reviewed and improved, including clearer information pathways and calls-to-action.

### Evaluation
Future analytics can be used to review page engagement, navigation behaviour and interactions with important calls-to-action.

## Finding 2: Branded search visibility

The available Google search data showed strong performance for searches including "shallion support" and "shallion".

### Interpretation
The organisation has a strong foundation for branded search visibility, while broader non-branded search visibility provides an opportunity for future development.

### Action
Website content and service information were reviewed to improve clarity and strengthen the information available to users and search engines.

### Evaluation
Future Google Search performance can be compared using impressions, clicks, click-through rate and average position.

## Finding 3: Mobile website use

A substantial proportion of website sessions occurred on mobile devices.

### Interpretation
Important information and navigation need to remain usable across different screen sizes.

### Action
Updated website pages were checked across desktop and mobile views, with adjustments made to layout, spacing, readability and navigation.

### Evaluation
Future analytics and usability checks can be used to assess whether mobile users continue to engage effectively.

## Finding 4: Recorded form submissions

No form submissions were recorded during the period analysed.

### Interpretation
This finding does not demonstrate that no enquiries were received because users may have contacted Shallion through telephone or email.

### Action
Contact pathways and calls-to-action were reviewed as part of the wider website improvement work.

### Evaluation
Future monitoring of form submissions and other enquiry pathways could provide better evidence of website conversion.
