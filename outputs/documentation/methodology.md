# Methodology

The project used an iterative approach combining website review, digital information review, website analytics and practical website development.

## Website and Content Review

Existing website pages and digital materials were reviewed to identify issues relating to content clarity, information structure, navigation, calls-to-action and usability.

## Information Flow Review

The potential user journey from discovering Shallion, making an enquiry and accessing support information was considered to understand how information moves through the digital system.

## Website Analytics

Available Wix Analytics data were reviewed to examine visitor activity, traffic sources, page behaviour and search performance. Aggregate data were used for the portfolio.

## Website Development

Findings from the review and analytics were used to prioritise practical website improvements. Selected changes were implemented in Wix, followed by desktop and mobile usability checks.

## Documentation

The project outputs, findings and recommendations were documented to provide a clear record of the work and support future evaluation and development.
