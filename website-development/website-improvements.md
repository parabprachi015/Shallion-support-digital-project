# Website Improvements

During the placement, I reviewed and improved the Shallion Support website to make information clearer, easier to navigate and more consistent.

## Main Areas of Development

### Homepage
Reviewed the homepage structure and improved the presentation of key information and pathways to relevant sections.

### Shallion Support
Developed and refined content to explain the support available and provide clearer routes for visitors seeking further information.

### Dementia Support
Developed a dedicated Dementia Support webpage with structured information about the service, the people it supports, the types of support available and how to make an enquiry.

### Support Us
Developed and organised information explaining ways people can support Shallion, including contribution and social enterprise information.

### FAQ
Reviewed and updated frequently asked questions to make information about support options and costs clearer.

### Navigation and Calls-to-Action
Reviewed navigation, links and button wording to help visitors move more easily between important pages and identify the next step.

### Responsive and Usability Checks
Reviewed the updated website across desktop and mobile views and made adjustments to improve layout, readability, spacing and presentation.

## Overall Outcome

The website development work aimed to create a clearer and more consistent digital experience while supporting easier access to Shallion's services, information and contact routes.
