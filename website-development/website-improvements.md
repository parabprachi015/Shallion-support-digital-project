# Website Improvements

During the placement, I reviewed and developed the Shallion Support website to make information clearer, easier to navigate and more consistent.

## Main Areas of Development

### Homepage
Reviewed and restructured the homepage to improve the presentation of key information and service pathways. A key development was making Shallion Dementia Support clearly visible alongside Shallion Support.

### Shallion Support
Developed a dedicated Shallion Support webpage from scratch to explain the support available and provide clearer routes for visitors seeking further information or making an enquiry.

### Dementia Support
Developed a dedicated Dementia Support webpage from scratch with structured information about the service, the people it supports, the types of support available and how to make an enquiry.

### About Us
Redesigned the existing About Us webpage to improve the organisation and presentation of information about Shallion Support. Changes included page structure, layout and content presentation.

### Support Us
Created a dedicated Support Us webpage to explain how members of the public can support Shallion and how contributions relate to the organisation's wider support and social-enterprise activities.

### Shop
Created a dedicated Shop webpage from scratch to provide a clearer and more structured presentation of Shallion-related products and social-enterprise content.

### FAQ
Reviewed and updated frequently asked questions to make information about support options and costs clearer.

### Navigation and Calls-to-Action
Reviewed navigation, links and button wording to help visitors move more easily between important pages and identify appropriate next steps.

### Responsive and Usability Checks
Reviewed updated website pages across desktop and mobile views and made adjustments to improve layout, readability, spacing, consistency and presentation.

## Overall Outcome

The website development work aimed to create a clearer and more consistent digital experience while supporting easier access to Shallion's services, information, support pathways and contact routes.
